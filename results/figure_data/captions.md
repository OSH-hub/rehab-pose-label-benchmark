# Figure and table captions (MDPI style)

All values are computed by `make_figures.py` from the result files listed in `README.md`. Unless stated otherwise, points and bars are means over 6 seeds, error bars are ±1 SD over seeds, and 95% confidence intervals (CI) are percentile seed-bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples) of the mean of per-seed values; paired differences are formed within seed before resampling. The normalized label-efficiency AUC is the trapezoidal area of mAP@[.50:.95] versus label budget divided by the budget range (0.05–0.40). All models are YOLO11n trained cold-start for 30 epochs at 416 px on Camera17; budget 0.05 is the shared 5% ground-truth (GT) initialization.

## Figure R1 — `fig_strengthened_suite`

**Figure R1.** Strengthened GT-free self-training does not recover the pseudo-label collapse. (**a**) Label-efficiency curves (mAP@[.50:.95] on the Camera17 validation set) for the original pseudo-label arm A, the two strengthened GT-free arms (A_jointmask: joint-confidence masking at the frozen per-round 25th percentile of training-pool joint confidence; A_flipcons: additionally requiring horizontal-flip consensus within 5% of the image diagonal), the simulated-correction arm B (σ = 0) and the full-GT comparator ref. (**b**) Normalized label-efficiency AUC with 95% seed-bootstrap CI; the percentage inside each bar is the closure of the A-to-ref gap (mean over seeds of the per-seed ratio) with its 95% CI. The strengthened arms close only 6% [−1, 13] (A_jointmask) and 1% [−10, 14] (A_flipcons) of the gap, whereas B closes 97% [94, 101]; the dotted line marks the ref AUC. n = 6 seeds per arm.

## Figure R2 — `fig_sameframe_e1`

**Figure R2.** Same-frame label-source factorial: the collapse is caused by the labels, not by frame selection. For each seed and round the training frames are fixed to the frame lists actually used by ref (837/1,673/3,347 frames at budgets 0.10/0.20/0.40) and only the label source is swapped: GT labels (= ref), GT with additive N(0, 20 px) coordinate noise, pseudo-labels from the frozen 5%-GT initial model, and pseudo-labels from the self-updating A teacher (previous-round A model); no selection, rejection or replenishment is applied. The original adaptive A (its own selected frames) is shown as a dashed grey reference. (**a**) Camera17 (training view); (**b**) Camera18 (unseen view). Text boxes give the seed-paired difference versus GT labels at budget 0.40 with 95% seed-bootstrap CI. On the same frames, 20-px label noise costs −0.049 [−0.059, −0.039] mAP@[.50:.95], whereas either pseudo-label source costs −0.467 to −0.495, the same magnitude as the original A-versus-ref gap (−0.508 [−0.537, −0.482]) that also includes adaptive frame selection. Mean ± SD over 6 seeds.

## Figure R3 — `fig_crossview`

**Figure R3.** Cross-view robustness of Camera17-trained checkpoints evaluated, without retraining, on the training view (**a**, Camera17) and on the unseen portrait-orientation Camera18 (**b**; same subjects, recordings and frame indices, 2D GT projected to Camera18; inference at the 416-px training resolution). Boxes report the B closure of the A-to-ref AUC gap and the B−ref AUC difference with 95% seed-bootstrap CI: 97% [94, 101] and −0.010 [−0.022, +0.001] on Camera17 versus 87% [82, 93] and −0.020 [−0.029, −0.011] on Camera18. On Camera18 the pseudo-label arm A stays flat (0.074 → 0.075) as the budget grows eightfold. (**c**) Relative drop in mAP@[.50:.95] from Camera17 to Camera18 per checkpoint (paired within seed; mean ± SD over 6 seeds): the drop shrinks with label quality and budget, from 83% at the shared initialization to 56% for ref at budget 0.40.

## Figure R4 — `fig_downstream_c17_c18`

**Figure R4.** Downstream kinematic fidelity on Camera17 (filled bars) and the unseen Camera18 (hatched bars) for A, B and ref at each label budget, YOLO11n: (**a**) range-of-motion (ROM) error and (**b**) joint-angle mean absolute error (MAE), both in degrees over eight image-plane joint angles (lower is better; mean ± SD over 6 seeds, population SD). Both views were scored with the same post-correction evaluation code (Camera17: corrected run of 2026-08-09, `downstream_11n_u30_c17_corrected_2026-08-09/`; Camera18: `downstream_11n_u30_c18/`). On Camera17 at budget 0.40, B reduces joint-angle MAE from 19.4° (A) to 12.2° (seed-paired B−A −7.1° [−8.2, −6.1]) and ROM error from 37.2° to 18.1° (−19.1° [−23.2, −16.5]), close to ref (10.4° and 19.7°). On the unseen Camera18 the ROM advantage of the GT-derived arms persists (A 54.3°, B 33.5°, ref 30.5°; B−A −20.8° [−27.9, −15.3]), whereas joint-angle MAE differences narrow to 1–2° (A 23.6°, B 22.0°, ref 21.8°; B−A −1.6° [−3.9, +2.1]) because the portrait view distorts the image-plane angle computation. These metrics quantify kinematic fidelity on the held-out benchmark; they are not clinical outcome measures.

## Supplementary Figure S1 — `fig_resolution_sensitivity`

**Figure S1.** Inference-resolution sensitivity of models trained at 416 px; this is a sensitivity analysis, not a fairness comparison between views. (**a**) ref (seed 0, budget 0.40) evaluated on Camera18 at inference sizes 416/512/640/800 px with rectangular (rect = True, open markers) and square (rect = False, filled markers) letterboxing: pose mAP@[.50:.95] falls from 0.327 (416, rect) to 0.145 (512), 0.004–0.008 (640) and 0.000 (800), while box mAP@50 remains 0.93 at 512 px and persons are still detected in 199/200 frames at 640 px, i.e., localization precision rather than detection collapses once the person scale leaves the training distribution. (**b**, **c**) Label-efficiency curves of A/B/ref at 416 px (saturated) versus 640 px (pale, open markers) on Camera17 and Camera18 (mean ± SD over 6 seeds). At 640 px Camera17 ref drops from 0.81 to 0.74 at budget 0.40 and all Camera18 curves are ≈ 0. The main cross-view results are therefore reported at the 416-px training resolution.
